Privacy policy

Last updated: October 3, 2026

Photos and files

FormReady Pro uses the Android system photo picker to open only the image you choose. The app processes that image on your device. It does not upload your photo or signature to a server.

When you choose Download, FormReady saves the JPG you created to the Pictures/FormReady folder. The app does not keep a separate copy of the original image. You can remove exported files using your device’s photo or file app.

Information collection

FormReady Pro has no account, advertising, analytics, or tracking features. The Android app requests no internet access and does not collect or share personal information.

Children

FormReady Pro is a general utility and does not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

If the app’s data practices change, this policy will be updated before the changed practices are released.
